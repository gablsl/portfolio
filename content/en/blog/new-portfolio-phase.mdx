description: "I rebuilt my portfolio using local MDX files, i18n, and a darker redesign. Here's what changed and why."
date: "2025-10-30"
tags: ["nextjs", "mdx", "i18n", "portfolio"]

After some time using a CMS to manage my portfolio content, I decided to move everything to local MDX files. The goal is to have more control over the code, make versioning easier, and make deployments more predictable.

I also took the opportunity to add support for two languages (Portuguese and English), reorganize the experiences section into an alternating timeline, and darken the color palette with a more refined emerald tone. I also created the Blog and About pages, which had been in the plans for a while.

In upcoming posts, I plan to share more about technical decisions, learnings, and ongoing projects.
