The project was developed during the React.js 2022 Ignite track challenge. During this challenge, we used more advanced concepts to build a coffee ordering app from scratch! The app has 3 screens: the first is a presentation screen and a list of available coffees. On the second screen, the user fills out a form with their address, and on the right side they can view cart items, change quantities, or remove them. On the third and final screen, the order confirmation and delivery details are displayed.
